Project Refinement: Responsible AI Deployment Checklist

As part of my Week 4 project refinement, I revisited my AI and cloud coursework through the lens of the ethical dilemmas discussed in class — including Apple's CSAM detection proposal, Google's location tracking, and AirTag misuse. The recurring pattern I observed was that companies address ethics reactively. I chose to refine my project proactively by building ethical consideration into the deployment process itself. Before deploying any AI model, I now document the following:

  1. Data provenance and consent: What data is being used, and was consent obtained? Is the data appropriate for this use?
  2. Risk allocation: Who bears the risk if the model produces harmful or inaccurate outputs — the user, the organization, or a third party?
  3. Human oversight: What human review mechanism exists if the model fails, and how quickly can it be intervened in?
  4. Transparency: Can the model's decisions be explained to the people affected by them?
  5. Repurposing risk: Could this system be repurposed for surveillance or harm beyond its intended use, and what safeguards prevent that?
